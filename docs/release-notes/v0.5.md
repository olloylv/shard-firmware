**Unofficial custom firmware for the Erica Synths LXR Eurorack module. Not made, approved or supported by Erica Synths. Install at your own risk; the original firmware can be restored at any time.**

SHARD keeps the LXR panel and workflow and lets you put any of 23 sound engines on any of the six channels: LXR classics, Plaits percussion and physical models, Machinedrum-inspired engines and IDM/glitch engines.

### New in 0.5

- Kit banks: up to 100 banks of 128 kits, Load Bank / Save Bank on the LOAD/SAVE button, the last bank comes back at power-on
- Five banks, 640 kits: 00 factory, 01 techno, 02 electro, 03 psytrance, 04 industrial
- Ring modulator on every channel (internal carrier or another channel's output)
- Ring modulator FX (`rng`) is back next to the glitch: six carriers, AM bias, stereo spread, random carrier per hit
- Float mix bus, soft limiter and 24-bit DAC output

Kits from 0.4 load unchanged (ring off).

Full list: [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/olloylv/shard-firmware/blob/shard/CHANGELOG.md)

### Install

1. Copy `LXRE_SHARD_V0.5.img` to the root of a FAT32 SD card (remove other `LXRE*.img` files) and the `shard` folder from the banks zip to the root as well. Banks 00–04 on the card are replaced, so back up your own first.
2. Power off, insert the card, hold the encoder, power on; keep holding until "LXR Updater" appears.
3. Wait for "Update OK!". The start-up screen shows `SHARD v0.5`.

Already on SHARD: global menu → **Upd** → pick the file → confirm.

Back to stock: put the official `LXRE*.img` from Erica Synths on the card and use the bootloader the same way.
